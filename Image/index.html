TAP-BD: Turbulence-aware Poisson blind deconvolution
in photon-starved scenarios

Seungman Choi Peter Menart Andrew Schramka Shubhanka Jape Leif Bauer Zubin Jacob*

* Corresponding author: zjacob@purdue.edu

PAPER CODE LAB WEBSITE

📄 Abstract

Imaging distant objects through Earth’s atmosphere is especially difficult when the air is turbulent and only a few photons reach the sensor. Traditional correction methods often break down in these cases because they can’t handle strong distortions or extreme low light. We introduce TAP-BD, a new method designed for such harsh conditions. It uses random phase patterns to encode more information from each image and applies a customized Poisson denoiser to remove noise caused by low photon counts. Then, using an iterative optimization algorithm (Alternating direction method of multipliers; ADMM), TAP-BD simultaneously recovers both the object and the distortion caused by turbulence. Even when the turbulence is severe, TAP-BD reconstructs clear images with strong accuracy—demonstrating its power for photon-starved imaging in astronomy and beyond.
